## Our Current Global State & History


{{< figure src="/images/blog/windows95.jpg" alt="windows" width="600" caption="Launch day of Microsoft Windows 95" >}}

Think of where the world sits currently, Big Data surging, incredible medical breakthroughs every year, continuation of social and geopolitical justice. Now, in 2026, we have supposed access to instant information at our fingertips whenever and wherever we want, in a personalized configuration through the implementation of Artificial Intelligence. Through endless training and data mining AI is experiencing an exponential trend of explosive advancement with the world struggling to keep up with it, scrambling to understand the moral and ethical implications of this ever-growing technology. Let's scale this entire conversation back a bit, on July 14, 1995, Microsoft released the first consumer-oriented operating system, without the dependent foundation of the MS-DOS framework, Windows 95. This completely broke down the wall of technological possibility, as with previous installations of Windows the user was presented with a graphical shell essentially layered over the MS-DOS operating system, omitting Windows NT. The release of Windows 95 was a major catalyst in the progression of the internet also, as whilst it did not allow for TCP/IP at its launch it did provide key trailblazing network features such as DHCPP and dial-up networking through then popular services like AOL.

If we take into consideration the presence of accelerating change in respect to technology, it is our responsibility as humans to adapt consequentially to our own creations, and mandatory when our technological advancements create divide or confusion in other parts of our lives. I think it is worthwhile to ponder how different areas of our world adjust to society's lightspeed developments, as in many cases it can place a country within an unfavorable situation. For example, in May 2021, a ransomware group called DarkSide gained access to Colonial Pipeline, the largest products pipeline in the entire United States which transports over 100 million gallons of fuel every day across the entire East Coast. The attack was so severe that it resulted in the Biden Administration declaring a state of emergency. DarkSide accessed the network with relative ease as the system did not have multifactor identification protocols in place. As a result, the pipeline shutdown spanned over a 5-day period between May 7-12 of 2021. The ransom of the access was set at 75 bitcoin, approximately worth $4.4 million. The Department of Justice recovered 3/4ths of the ransom, but the company experienced severe reputational decline as it is highly discouraged by the FBI to provide such payments to these types of organizations.

Shortly after the attack, various plaintiffs in the class action lawsuit sued Colonial Pipeline for negligence, in which the statement assessed that the incident was responsible for the negative impact towards 11,000 fuel retailers across the country. While the suit was unsuccessful, the cost expenditure was monumental for the company, and during the brutal aftermath the US Department of Transportation issued a compliance order which mandated Colonial Pipeline a civil penalty of $1 million USD.


## Societal Necessity

The way technology has polarized the globe over the past 20 years is remarkable, with a portion of the world population believing that technology should be strictly controlled under ethical laws pertaining to personal data and should have no place in authoritative roles. The way that technology grows is not only global however, as one can make the case that modern technology must also fit the mould of the system it occupies. For instance, In places like China there exists a semi-reliant nature for Artificial Intelligence to operate within China's titanic infrastructure. This reliance manifests as Industrial and Government Integration, as China is pushing towards a infusion of AI into core sectors including manufacturing, finance, logistics and public administration. Arguably most interesting however, is the full-fledged use of AI in the healthcare sector. Specifically, the Zhongshan Hospital in Shanghai is developing an AI programmed to act as a pilot which aggregates medical and personal information to assess and recommend certain healthcare and lifestyle interventions to individuals, and even more entire hospitals across China. Additionally, the growth and importance of AI in China allows them to create distinct AI-driven products and services which other regions are yet to explore. In fact, on July 26th, 2025, Chinese Premier Li Qiang unveiled China's comprehensive 13-point plan of Action for AI Governance at the World Artificial Intelligence Conference. 

The Plan of Action contained aspects concerning the national relationship with AI, as Li Qiang described the rapid development and monetization of AI as an "exclusive game" in which only a few players will have a stake in the sector. Within the plans' governance framework, Open-source development is a major point of focus as the plan advocates for building reliable open-source platforms to lower the floor for technological innovation and application. In turn, enhancing the inclusiveness of AI services across the country.
